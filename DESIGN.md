# AeroDub Studio 0.5 direction

0.5.2 lyric readings: the owner requests Latin readings for Japanese, Mandarin and Korean. Original text remains the main lyric in the default view. A labelled display selector switches between original, original plus reading, and reading only. The secondary reading uses smaller Segoe UI text on the existing pale stage; it follows source lyric timing and never obscures the original. The lyric editor provides a reading field for correction and an automatic-reading action. Existing Aero colors, focus styles and ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 remain. These are reading aids, not translated lyrics; no new visual asset is introduced.

0.5.1 singing timing: the owner reports early/late lyrics and a late voice in recorded covers. A collapsed Timing adjustments section beside the existing recording controls exposes two distinct values: song-wide lyric timing and alignment of the selected recording. Labels state milliseconds and the earlier/later sign convention. Preview line sits with each lyric row so timing can be checked against the audio. Existing Segoe UI, opaque pale work surfaces and blue accents keep the ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 direction; this correction adds no decoration or animation. Controls wrap within the desktop minimum width and lock during recording. Actual captured samples and exported audio determine timing, rather than a cosmetic change to the playhead.

0.5.0 Professional mode: the owner supplied a CapCut timeline reference and requested manual alignment alongside the existing Easy workflow. The full-width timeline places video, original audio and persistent numbered dub tracks on one ruler. Selected clips use a blue outline; dub waveforms use the existing muted teal to distinguish voice content from original audio. Track names, arming and mute indicate real recording/mix state. A solid pale inspector at the right contains Timing and Audio tabs; selecting audio opens its actual clip effects. The EQ response graph is functional data, with frequency/dB axes and no decorative spectrum. The preview can enter fullscreen while retaining playback, clock and seek controls. Existing Segoe UI, Aero mountains/bubbles, reflective pressed controls, solid work surfaces and ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 remain. At narrow widths preview and inspector stack, and the timeline scrolls inside its own region. The status is in page flow in Professional mode so it cannot cover clips. No reference branding or external visual asset is copied.

0.4.2 low microphone input: one saved Auto microphone level checkbox above the existing voice sliders makes automatic input leveling discoverable and optional. It reuses current check/label/hint styles in all five languages, with a brief explanation about quiet microphones and input dynamics. The checkbox locks while recording. No new panel, decoration, animation or palette change; ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 stay consistent. Capture/cleanup changes address actual audio level rather than increasing the meter display.

0.4.1 music preview: the owner wants to hear isolated instruments, isolated vocals or both before entering Sing it! and exporting. Three adjacent text buttons replace the singer checkbox in the prepared-song player. The selected button uses a restrained white-to-pale-blue reflection, a blue outline and check mark to read as a pressed Aero control. The same selection drives the export dropdown, so listening and export choices agree; Both exports separate tracks in a ZIP. Play preview gets a standard play/pause symbol next to its label. Singing keeps its own original-vocal guide toggle and full instrumental. Existing Segoe UI, solid work surfaces, spacing and ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 stay consistent; no new decorative asset or animation is added.

0.4.0 voice and cut refinement: the owner requests shared own-voice controls and a listening toggle in Settings. Five labelled sliders use the existing pale panel and blue accent, with aligned numeric outputs, one monitor checkbox and a reset action. Settings scrolls so microphone choice and voice adjustments remain reachable at the desktop minimum size. Processing changes the user's actual microphone preview and future captured takes in both rooms. A collapsed Adjust this part section holds start/end, split at video position and join-next controls before recording; the main record/check/retake focus remains clear. Speech cores replace background-filled/equal-length cuts, and local change consensus supplements voice-group clustering. All messages support the five existing languages. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 stay unchanged.

0.3.1 smart dub refinement: the owner asks for cuts at speaker changes as well as quiet gaps, and for Listen to your take to play the video alongside the voice. Analysis runs on entering Dubbing mode, with the existing cancellable progress surface. Small numbered speaker labels extend each existing part row; they reflect actual local analysis, not named identities. Audition follows the shifted/trimmed take's video position without original-audio doubling. Existing recordings lock cuts to preserve alignment. The blue Aero layout, visual hierarchy and motion dials stay consistent.

0.3.0 music direction: the owner requests MP3 separation, stem export, editable lyrics in every supported language and a pleasant Sing it! room. Home now offers video dubbing and music as two explicit entry points. Prepared songs give listening/export controls one plane and transcription/Sing it! the other; a line editor follows them. The singing room keeps large synchronized lyrics at its center, previous/next lines for context, a real 16-bar spectrum and clear record/listen/retake/export controls. Pale work surfaces, dark text and existing blue mountains/bubbles retain the Aero theme. Spectrum movement is capped at 30px, respects reduced motion, and reflects playing audio. A single 550ms entrance transition marks entering the room. The owner rejected the stronger portal sound, previewed a softer synthesized version and explicitly approved it. Settings can disable or preview that 1.8-second soft sound. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2 remain unchanged.

0.2.3 refinement: the owner requested water-wave VFX inside the welcome glass panel, plus a better language picker and Mandarin, Japanese and Korean. Three translucent wave layers and sparse light ripples move slowly within that existing panel; the caption stays still and readable. Motion stops for app/OS reduced-motion settings and pauses off the welcome screen. Language rows reuse the microphone menu's pale blue selected state, show native language names, support the keyboard and persist selection. Existing dubbing controls keep their layout.

0.2.2 refinement: the owner requested a more minimal icon. The same blue glass orb now contains one bold white sound wave; landscape and decorative details are removed so the mark reads in the taskbar. The interface and dubbing flow retain their existing behavior.

0.2.1 refinement: the project menu is a searchable list with meaningful saved metadata and separate delete controls. Microphone choices move into Settings and use a matching selected-row menu. Home is an explicit top-level action. The header remains at the top of the viewport and reserves space for native Windows controls using matching pale blue. Icon direction changes from the rejected corporate microphone tile to a richly rendered glass/nature/audio-wave Frutiger Aero asset. See icon-direction.md for the generated concept and prompt.

User direction: keep the Frutiger aesthetic, modernize it, and make the workflow easy to understand. The owner clarified the interaction as upload -> enter dubbing mode -> record a smart-cut part -> listen/retake -> next part -> full review -> export or return to a particular part. Later direction explicitly asks for a blue backdrop, animated Aero bubbles and mountains. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

- Color: the owner-requested blue sky backdrop, pale sky work surfaces and a lake-blue primary action keep the Aero mood while replacing the heavy navy/chrome treatment the owner rejected.
- Material: one frosted header, an original CSS ocean-window motif and original SVG mountain layers supply the requested water/landscape identity. Opaque pale surfaces keep text readable over the backdrop. No external assets are copied.
- Type: installed Segoe UI Variable uses contemporary Windows typography without redistributing font files; regular weights and less ornament replace the nostalgic chrome look.
- Layout: upload has one main action; prepared video has one Start dubbing action; dubbing shows the part list, actual video/waveforms, and a focused record/check/retake panel. Full review gives the video more space.
- Spacing: roomy video/record areas carry the task; take alignment and trims are collapsed because they are secondary decisions.
- Controls: a prominent percentage slider raises all dubbed voices to 400%; original volume remains separate. Normal buttons have 10px corners; larger media/record planes use 18px, waveform surfaces 6px.
- Workflow: recording stops at the current part boundary and stays on the part. Next is explicit. Review retains the part list so any part can be retaken without restarting the project.
- Motion: the owner explicitly requests slow ambient bubbles. Seven sparse bubbles use only transforms and opacity behind noninteractive backdrop surfaces; no movement covers video or blocks controls. Reduced motion hides bubbles and disables transitions. The mountains and ocean-window motif stay static.
- Selection: Ctrl+A is blocked on the noneditable app surface. Text/number fields keep native selection; the copyable status area selects only its own content.

Interaction reference, checked on the creator's official site: https://yeahmaybe.itch.io/the-choicer-voicer/devlog/1453484/version-050-released . Its clip-by-clip dubbing/review interaction informed the flow. No screenshots, character imagery, scores, game-show UI, or third-party assets are copied into AeroDub.
