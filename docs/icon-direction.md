## Previous icon, 0.2.1

The owner rejected the microphone tile as bland and overly professional. The replacement uses the built-in imagegen tool and an explicitly requested vivid Frutiger Aero material language.

Concepts considered: a nature-filled glass orb organized by water/sound; a refracting audio disc organized by playback; a miniature sky window organized by the dubbing scene. The orb keeps the strongest Aero identity and gives the sound ripple a natural relation to the app's voice workflow. The disc risked looking like a generic media-player logo; the window risked remaining a flat application tile.

Selected: a blue glass orb holding a lush lake/mountain/cloud world, with water forming the sound-wave signature. No text or microphone glyph; PNG transparency is retained. Small Windows icon variants are derived from the generated asset.

Asset: `src/renderer/app-icon.png`. Windows multi-size icon: `src/renderer/app-icon.ico`. Tool: built-in image_gen. Original generated output remains in the Codex generated-images directory.

Final prompt:

> Create one original desktop application icon for AeroDub Studio. The feeling is joyful, lush, nostalgic Frutiger Aero: a tiny world of blue sky, fresh green hills and clear water held inside glossy translucent blue-aqua glass. Make the glass feel rounded, richly refractive and tactile, with beautiful white reflections, luminous water and a few soft clouds. A flowing sound ripple or audio-wave gesture belongs naturally to the water, giving this voice-dubbing app its own identity. This should feel like a lovingly rendered mid-2000s multimedia icon with depth and vivid color, while still reading clearly at Windows taskbar size. Choose the internal arrangement freely. One strong recognizable silhouette with generous transparent space outside the object, centered square composition. No text, letters, watermark, microphone glyph, flat corporate symbol, square app tile or copied Windows logo. Generate the icon itself, not a screenshot or presentation board. Actual transparent background.
